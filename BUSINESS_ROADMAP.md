## 1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW

ScopeGuard is a specialized micro-SaaS and professional utility engineered to solve one of the most painful, financially draining problems in the freelance and digital agency economy: **unbilled scope creep and out-of-scope client revisions**.

- **Annual financial leak per freelancer:** $4,850+ in unbilled "quick favor" tweaks.
- **Core Product:** Interactive Scope Creep Loss Calculator + Automated Legal Change Order Amendment Generator + Pre-Project Scope Risk Diagnostic + Tactical Email Negotiation Scripts.
- **Zero-Cost Footprint:** 100% static client-side architecture (HTML5/CSS3/Vanilla JS), local storage persistence, zero cloud compute costs, zero database upkeep, printable vector PDF output.
